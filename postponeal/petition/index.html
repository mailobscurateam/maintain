Action Campaign regarding the Postponed 2026 G.C.E. A/L Examination

We, the G.C.E. Advanced Level candidates, parents, and educators of Sri Lanka, formally appeal to the Department of Examinations regarding the critical impacts of the Postponed 2026 A/L Exam schedule.

Postponing the G.C.E. A/L National Examinations deeply disrupts high school academic calendars, causes extensive delays in entering state or international universities, and places a severe psychological and mental burden on thousands of candidates preparing to face this crucial life milestone.

We seek a swift, transparent, and structured resolution to the revised examination dates to ensure candidates can plan their academic future with confidence and certainty.

Note: Keeping candidates updated on exact scheduling timeline rules ensures procedural discipline, fair opportunities, and protects university admissions timelines nationwide.
